## 3. Interpretation

- Raw context remains the utility ceiling but fails redaction/audit gates because it keeps synthetic canaries and has no source-span schema.
- MemoryFrame has the best auditability and safety behavior, but its source-span metadata is token-expensive on short fixtures.
- Compact JSON is often shorter than MemoryFrame while preserving facts, confirming the M3 risk that JSON can beat a human-readable DSL on raw token count.
- Local JSONL retrieval is the strongest P0 baseline when the downstream question targets only a subset of facts.
- TokenCompressor's rough prose optimizer is useful as a candidate generator, but by itself it lacks exact-token provenance, redaction, and source-span guarantees.
- The S2 executable fixture exposes a design gap: fact-style compression can preserve expected stdout but does not necessarily preserve runnable code. Future MemoryFrame variants need an explicit executable-artifact mode or must score execution separately from fact recall.
